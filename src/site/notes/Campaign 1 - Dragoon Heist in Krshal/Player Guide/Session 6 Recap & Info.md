Carrying on in the Blue Alley, the party retraced their steps and found a secret wine storage room where they found 5 bottles of vintage Amnish wine. After some dwarvish smashing of barrels, they also discovered a hidden golden door with a coin slot on its face. They tried to pry it open with a normal gold piece, but nothing happened. Moving onwards, the party next came across a huge chamber with floating wooden platforms hanging from massive steel chains anchored in the ceiling above. Filled with smoke, the other side of the chamber wasn't visible so Petulia used her shape shift ability to turn into a Tressym and attempted to fly across. As she moved through the chamber, smoke mephits flung themselves at her out of the mists below but she managed to flee them before they could strike. Landing on the final platform, Petulia found a Sun Blade longsword, before leaping to safety on the other side of the chamber. The rest of the party, not wanting to risk the smoke mephits attacking them, decided to peel back around to the start of the dungeon where they came to a corridor with two doorways labelled 'The High Way' and 'The Low Way'. They also noticed a skull embossed into the far end wall with a small unassuming button recessed into the eye socket of the skull. Pressing it, Pussuid revealed a secret doorway leading them into another corridor. Following their new path through the double doors at the end, they came across a shabby but resplendent reception room. Here they managed to pilfer a magical abacus, which they surmised would help them to keep track of their tavern's profits and losses. Off on the east wall of the room, the party opened doors onto a statue of Keiler himself, the Wizard who built this place long ago. After some prodding and poking, the statue spoke to the party and asked them if they had a riddle for him?! After some mental rearranging, the party spoke "What might and strength cannot get through, this tool with a soft touch can do" which they had pieced together from random fragments dotted around the Alley. The statue dropped an emerald encrusted wand at their feet and ceased to speak to them. Moving on through the northern doors, they entered another corridor and reunited with Petulia, back in elf form! At first, they went into the first door on the left which led to an alchemical lab of sorts, with assorted trinkets and potions strewn across the myriad workbenches. At the far end of the room, Gloom spied what appeared to a very special healing potion. The party sent Pussuid in to retrieve the potion, but as she made her way up and down the edge of the workshop she knocked over several vials which literally blew up in her face. Once back in the safety of the corridor outside, Petulia determined that the healing potion was an 'all life healing potion' - a rare item indeed! The next door down on this wall, led them to a pit where two magmins sat at the bottom incinerating trash. Not being able to communicate in their language, the party moved on. They next entered a room littered with copious gold coins. Unfortunately, they determined the coins were laid out to spell the words 'all fake' and they decided to leave well enough alone. Moving on up the corridor, they entered the next room which had a golden path laid out on the tiled floor in front of them. Unfortunately, stepping off the path caused several spectres from the mural on the wall to literally step off the walls around them. Luckily, Kween's Sun Blade dealt enough radiant damage to the undead spirits to see them off quickly. This allowed the party to enter the adjacent room which was completely mirrored but which held the unicorn they had been sent in to retrieve. Leaving with the unicorn, they retraced their steps and decided to rest over night in the reception room. Exiting back into the first corridor, they again decided to send Petulia, in Tressym form, across the High Way path in order to avoid the pressure plates on each path they'd detected. Not Wanting to risk, setting off any traps, the party decided to use their rope of holding, throwing it across the spiked pit in the Low Way corridor. Catching it, Petulia braced herself whilst Kween tied the rope to the door handles on the other side of the pit. Climbing across, Petulia lost her grip and Kween slipped into the pit below but she only took minimal damage and managed to climb up to the other side and take over from Petulia whilst Gloom and Pussuid made their way across the rope with no further trouble. All on the other side of the passageways, they saw a giant silver door which they opened with the key they had found in the very first room of the Alley. Entering a chamber on the other side strewn with guts, bones, blood, and gore, the party immediately stepped into bear traps that simultaneously released 4 skeletons into the chamber. Fighting them off took some time, but finally the party destroyed the skeletons and Pussuid used her strength to free herself and then the others. Looking into the small room where the skeletons came from, the party managed to prize some semi-precious gemstones from the skull alter there. Returning to the chamber, and taking great care to avoid anymore bear traps, the party looked through to the next corridor which was filled with swinging saw blades. Pussuid made the choice to move slowly through the corridor, taking her time to avoid the blades as she went, and, reaching the other end, opened the door to see a singular golden coin on a plush red pillow in the centre of the circular, domed room. Returning with the coin, Pussuid and the gang returned to the wine room they first found and inserted the coin into the slot on the Golden Door which revealed a lantern of revealing behind. Lastly, the party returned to the one unexplored room of the alleyway they missed before. Here, they found a giant set of iron gates and heard a booming voice say 'Beyond these gates lies paradise, enter them as you entered life, and you may ye find it; grasp the jewel and grasp the iron, then be whisked away to the truest of rewards'. Pussuid, taking the initiative, stripped naked, grasped the gemstone which the strange iron marking they found in the alley previously, and grabbed the iron gates. Instantly, she was teleported to the exit corridor completely naked and without the gemstone. Back in the room, the others saw her disappear before noticing the gemstone fall to the floor in front of them. Thinking better of stripping naked themselves, they also followed Pussuid through the gates and out of the Blue Alley. Returning to their home, the unicorn spoke to the party telepathically, letting them know she was in fact a real unicorn called Primara who had been trapped in this form a long time ago by Mirt, who had sent the party on the job originally. When they tried to tell Mirt they had been unsuccessful in their task, he saw through them immediately and vowed revenge on them for keeping him from his unicorn!
